One window for your study day, the topics you miss most, and your keyboard shortcuts. Built for Step 1 students using AnKing-style decks, and it works with any deck. Study Day Today's reviews and new cards next to your usual range, flagged H or L when they're outside it. Study ahead pulls upcoming reviews into a temporary deck when you have time to spare. Freeze pauses reviews for a break so they don't pile up. Also a forecast, your pace, and a history where every action can be undone. Trouble Spots Topics ranked by how often you miss them, grouped by First Aid section, Pathoma chapter, Boards & Beyond or Sketchy video, organ system, subdeck or any tag. Click a topic to see its cards, open them in the Browser, or export a study sheet. It never changes your cards. Keyboard Change any shortcut on the review screen, the deck overview or anywhere in Anki. Click an action and press a key; if the key is taken, swap it, move the old action to a spare key, or unbind it. Smart undo flips back to the question instead of undoing a review. It also adds review keys for AnKing cards: c reveals the next cloze and v reveals them all, p copies the image under the mouse (or right-click › Copy image), and g copies the card's text with the clozes filled in. Open next hint and Open all hints (First Aid, Bootcamp…) can be bound too. Also
  • A status bar on the Decks screen with what's left today and how long tomorrow will take.
  • Light, dark or Auto (follows night mode), and eleven color themes.
  • An About tab with a quick start.
  • Nothing leaves your computer. The add-on never connects to the internet.
Opening it The Shinjer's Study Desk link in Anki's top bar, the status bar on the Decks screen, or Tools › Shinjer's Study Desk… Coming from Study Day Control, Trouble Spots or Keybind Remapper? This add-on replaces all three. Disable them first (Tools › Add-ons › Toggle Enabled), install this one and restart. Your shortcuts and history are copied over once; then you can delete the old ones. Requirements Anki 24.11 or newer. Problems, ideas, source code Please report bugs on GitHub rather than in reviews, since I can't reply to reviews. Include your Anki version (Help › About).
